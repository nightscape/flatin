FLatin is a learning app for practicing Latin vocabulary, declensions and conjugations.

With this app you can:
* Practice declining Latin nouns
* Practice conjugating Latin verbs
* Train word classification
* Track your learning progress with the FSRS algorithm (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler)

The app uses intelligent spaced repetition to help you learn and retain Latin grammar effectively. All data is stored locally on your device - no internet connection required.

Perfect for students and anyone looking to improve their Latin skills.
