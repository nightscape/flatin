Learn Latin helps you practise the vocabulary and grammar of lessons 1 to 40 of a Latin school course.

What the app does:
• Build forms: decline nouns, conjugate verbs
• Tasks to identify, match and translate word forms
• Find and correct mistakes in forms
• After a wrong answer, the app shows step by step how the form is built
• Practise words and grammar in separate rounds; words you do not know well yet come back more often
• On the "Course position" page you set the lesson you have reached in your course. Then only matching words and topics come up.

A Roman town grows with you:
You earn coins for tasks you solve correctly. Spend them on buildings such as a house, a fountain, a temple or baths, and build your own town.

Privacy:
All data stays on the device. The app needs no account and no internet connection, shows no ads and sends nothing anywhere.

The app is available in German and English.
